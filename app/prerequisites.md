# Prerequisites

## Android

Android must be at least version 6 (Marshmallow) or higher.

You can download the app from here:

[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dbnetsoft.RemoteRedirect](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dbnetsoft.RemoteRedirect)

## iOS

iPhone and iPad must run iOS 15 or higher. USB-RS232-adapters are not supported on iOS, so you need a network converter or a [Bluetooth adapter](bluetooth-adapters.md).

You can download the app from here:

[https://apps.apple.com/app/remoteredirect/id6826857764](https://apps.apple.com/app/remoteredirect/id6826857764)

## USB-RS232-Adapter

The chip on the USB-RS232-adapter should be any of the following:

* FTDI FT232R, FT232H, FT2232H, FT4232H, FT230X, FT231X, FT234XD
* Prolific PL2303
